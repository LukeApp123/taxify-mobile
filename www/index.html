    Reduce your bill — legally

    £

    Pension contributions

    Pay into a personal pension to cut your taxable income. Contributions attract relief at your marginal rate, and keeping income under £100,000 preserves your full Personal Allowance.

    Annual allowance £60,000 (tapers above £260,000 adjusted income).
    ISA

    Shelter gains in an ISA

    Up to £20,000 a year in a Stocks & Shares ISA grows free of income tax and Capital Gains Tax — dividends and disposals inside the wrapper never touch your £3,000 CGT allowance or your tax bands. Direct cryptoassets generally can’t be held in an ISA, so shelter eligible shares and funds instead.

    “Bed & ISA”: realise gains within your £3,000 allowance, then hold future growth inside an ISA — repurchases made inside an ISA fall outside the 30-day matching rules.
    BTC

    Crypto & CGT relief

    HMRC taxes crypto as a chargeable asset (CGT, not income tax unless you’re trading). Offset losses against gains, use your £3,000 Annual Exempt Amount each year, and transfer assets to a spouse to double the allowance to £6,000 combined.

    Unused losses carry forward indefinitely — but you must claim them to HMRC (form SA108) within 4 years of the disposal; once claimed, they offset future gains.

    Claiming VAT back

    ✓

    What you can reclaim

    Stock and materials, tools and equipment, software and subscriptions, professional fees, business travel and fuel for business mileage, and a fair share of home office costs if you work from home.

    Keep VAT invoices. You can also reclaim VAT on some pre-registration purchases: goods bought in the last 4 years that you still have, and services in the last 6 months.
    ✕

    What you can't reclaim

    Anything for personal use, client entertainment, most cars, and goods or services used for exempt sales. Items used partly privately must be apportioned.

    If a purchase is 60% business use, reclaim 60% of the VAT.
    %

    Choosing a scheme

    Standard accounting counts VAT when you invoice. Cash accounting counts it when money moves, which helps if customers pay late — available up to £1.35 million turnover. The Flat Rate Scheme pays a fixed percentage of your gross turnover and skips most input VAT — you can join up to £150,000 and must leave above £230,000.

    This tool compares flat rate against standard accounting so you can see which costs less.
